Quick answers.

How much does cleaning cost?

Residential cleaning is $20.00 per hour. Job site cleanup and new home builds are priced per job, so we quote after a free consultation. Commercial work is quoted to suit the size of your space.

Which areas do you cover?

Edmonton, Fort Saskatchewan, St. Albert, Morinville and Leduc. If you are nearby and not sure, just ask.

How do I book?

Call (780) 862-9326 or send the form above. We work by appointment, are available 24/7, and the consultation is free.

Is it safe for electronics and delicate surfaces?

Yes. Our cleaners know the best treatment for each surface, and we promise that none of our cleaning treatments will damage your items.

What kinds of commercial cleaning do you offer?

Office cleaning, deep cleaning, pressure washing, waste management, carpet cleaning and more. Every service is cost-effective, and our cleaners are fully qualified.
